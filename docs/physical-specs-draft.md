# Open Capsule: Physical Specifications & Modular Blueprints (Draft v0.3)

## 1. Overview
This document defines the physical architecture of the Open Capsule. Unlike traditional time capsules, the Open Capsule is designed as a **Civilization Restart Kit**. It is not merely a container of data, but a modular system integrating digital intelligence, physical blueprints for machinery, and protocols for resource extraction and medicine production.

**Core Philosophy:** "Data tells you *what* to do; Blueprints tell you *how* to build it; Extraction Protocols tell you *where* to get the materials."

## 2. Modular Architecture
The Open Capsule is designed as a set of three interoperable modules. While Module 1 (Digital) is the core, Modules 2 and 3 provide the physical means for survival and reconstruction.

### Module 1: The Digital Core (The "Brain")
*Status: Defined in `architecture-draft.md`*
- **Function:** Two-stage AI system (Pilot + Main Core) + Archival Storage (Project Silica/M-Disc).
- **Content:** Encyclopedic knowledge, OSE blueprints index, chemical databases, medical guides.
- **Physical Form:** Faraday-shielded rack mounted inside the main container.

### Module 2: The Physical Library (The "Hands")
*Status: Concept Phase*
- **Function:** Redundant storage of critical blueprints in analog format (laser-etched stainless steel plates or sintered ceramic tiles).
- **Why Analog?** Immune to EMP, solar flares, and bit-rot. Readable with simple magnification.
- **Key Blueprints Included:**
  - **Open Source Ecology (OSE) Global Village Construction Set (GVCS):**
    - *Priority Machines:* LifeTrac (tractor), Power Cube (engine), CEB Press (brick maker), Induction Furnace (metal casting).
    - *Reference:* [OSE Machine Index](https://www.opensourceecology.org/gvcs/gvcs-machine-index/)
  - **Open Source Mining & Refining:**
    - *Micromining Principles:* In-situ processing, low-energy extraction (biomining with bacteria/acid), solar thermal reduction.
    - *Equipment:* Manual ore crushers, sluice boxes, basic electrolysis cells for copper/aluminum extraction.
    - *Reference:* [OSE Micromining](https://wiki.opensourceecology.org/wiki/Micromining)
  - **Bio-Lab & Pharmacy:**
    - *Equipment:* OpenCell (3-in-1 centrifuge/vortex/homogenizer), Dremelfuge (drill centrifuge), OpenPCR (thermal cycler), microscope (Foldscope design).
    - *Reference:* [BioBlocksLab](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36641111/), [OpenCell](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0298857)

### Module 3: The Starter Kit (The "Seeds")
*Status: Concept Phase*
- **Function:** Physical samples and tools to jumpstart Module 2.
- **Components:**
  - **Seed Bank:** Vacuum-sealed heirloom seeds (calibrated for local latitude, stored in the specific capsule).
  - **Chemical Reagents:** Stable precursors for basic antibiotics, analgesics, and antiseptics (e.g., precursors for aspirin, ethanol, basic sulfonamides).
  - **Tooling:** High-grade hand tools (files, saws, measuring instruments) made from tool steel resistant to corrosion.
  - **Extraction Guides:** Laminated flowcharts for extracting biochemical compounds from local flora (alkaloids, essential oils, tannins).

## 3. Detailed Specifications: Mining & Extraction
*Critical for hardware reconstruction and chemical synthesis.*

### 3.1 Basic Mining & Metallurgy
Survivors need to extract iron, copper, aluminum, and silicon to repair or build new hardware.
- **Protocols Included:**
  - **Biomining:** Using bacteria (e.g., *Acidithiobacillus*) to leach copper from low-grade ores. (Low energy, high time).
  - **Carbothermal Reduction:** Using charcoal (from biomass) to reduce iron oxide in a bloomery or small blast furnace.
  - **Electrolysis:** Simple setups for refining copper or extracting aluminum from clay/bauxite using hydrofluoric acid (with safety protocols).
- **Hardware Blueprints:**
  - Manual rock crushers (lever-based).
  - Solar concentrators for reaching smelting temperatures (>1500°C).
  - Bellows designs (human or water-powered).

### 3.2 Biochemical Extraction & Medicine Production
Survivors need to produce essential medicines and reagents from local plants and basic chemistry.
- **Protocols Included:**
  - **Solvent Extraction:** Step-by-step guides for using ethanol/water mixtures to extract alkaloids, flavonoids, and terpenes from medicinal plants.
  - **Distillation:** Plans for simple copper stills to purify ethanol (for antiseptics and fuel) and extract essential oils.
  - **Synthesis of Essentials:**
    - *Aspirin:* From willow bark (salicin) to acetylsalicylic acid.
    - *Antibiotics:* Basic fermentation protocols for Penicillin (with safety warnings on contamination) and extraction of antimicrobial compounds from garlic/honey/mold.
    - *Painkillers:* Extraction of morphine/codeine from poppies (strictly for medical use protocols).
  - **Lab Equipment DIY:**
    - *Centrifuge:* 3D-printed rotors for power drills (Dremelfuge).
    - *Microscope:* Lens grinding guides or Foldscope assembly.
    - *Reagents:* Making pH indicators from cabbage, iodine from seaweed.
- **Reference:** [WHO Essential Medicines List](https://medicalguidelines.msf.org/sites/default/files/pdf/guideline-339-en.pdf) (Included in full text in the Digital Core).

## 4. Structural Design & Materials
- **Container:** Modified ISO Shipping Container (20ft) or reinforced concrete monolith.
- **Shielding:**
  - *EMP/CME:* Copper mesh lining (continuous weld) + Steel outer shell.
  - *Water/Fire:* Sealed gaskets, flood barriers, fire-retardant internal coating.
- **Power Interface:**
  - External solar panel mounts (standardized connectors).
  - Internal battery bank vault (temperature-controlled).
- **Location:** Elevated ground (flood protection), near water source (cooling + hydro potential), proximity to raw materials (clay, ore, biomass).

## 5. Implementation Roadmap (Phase 3)
1.  **CAD Design:** Create 3D models of the container layout, rack systems, and shielding layers.
2.  **Material Testing:** Test steel plates for laser etching durability (corrosion resistance over 100+ years).
3.  **Blueprint Curation:** Select the top 20 most critical OSE machines and top 50 essential medicines for the "Priority List".
4.  **Extraction Validation:** Verify all chemical extraction protocols using only basic, low-tech equipment to ensure feasibility in a post-collapse scenario.

## 6. Collaboration Needs
- **Mechanical Engineers:** To adapt OSE designs for the "Starter Kit" scale.
- **Chemists/Pharmacists:** To simplify medicine synthesis protocols for low-resource settings.
- **Geologists/Metallurgists:** To create local ore identification guides and smelting charts.
- **Botanists:** To compile regional ethnobotany databases (medicinal plants by biome).
