# Open Capsule: Portable University Curriculum (Draft v1.0)

## 1. Vision & Mission
**Mission:** To provide a complete, self-guided educational pathway that can rebuild human technical capacity from post-collapse levels to advanced civilization within 1-2 generations.

**Core Philosophy:** "Knowledge without pedagogy is useless." The Open Capsule does not just store books; it provides a **structured curriculum** guided by an AI tutor, moving learners from basic literacy to professional competence in critical fields.

**Target Audience:** Survivors with varying levels of prior education, ranging from basic literacy to advanced technical aptitude.

## 2. Curriculum Architecture
The curriculum is divided into **Four Tiers**, designed to be sequential but modular.

### Tier 1: Foundations (The "New Basics")
*Goal: Restore basic literacy, numeracy, and scientific reasoning.*
- **Subjects:**
  - **Literacy & Languages:** Reading, writing, and critical analysis. (Source: *Project Gutenberg*, *Global Literature Archive*).
  - **Mathematics:** Arithmetic → Algebra → Geometry → Calculus. (Source: *Khan Academy Offline*, *OpenStax Mathematics*).
  - **Scientific Method:** Logic, observation, hypothesis testing.
  - **Digital Literacy:** How to use the Capsule's AI and interfaces.
- **Format:** Interactive text, audio lessons (for low-energy modes), and AI-guided exercises.

### Tier 2: Survival & Stability (Immediate Post-Collapse)
*Goal: Ensure immediate survival, health, and food security.*
- **Subjects:**
  - **Medicine I (Primary Care):** Triage, wound care, infectious disease control, basic pharmacology. (Source: *WHO Essential Medicines*, *MSF Medical Guidelines*, *Where There Is No Doctor*).
  - **Agriculture:** Soil regeneration, crop rotation, seed saving, pest management. (Source: *FAO Open Access*, *OSE Agriculture*).
  - **Water & Sanitation:** Purification, well digging, waste management. (Source: *UNICEF WASH Manuals*).
  - **Construction:** Shelter building using local materials (adobe, timber, stone). (Source: *OSE CEB Press Manual*).
- **AI Role:** Diagnostic assistant ("Describe symptoms..."), crop advisor ("Identify this leaf spot...").

### Tier 3: Reconstruction (The "Technical Core")
*Goal: Rebuild infrastructure, energy, and industry.*
- **Subjects:**
  - **Engineering I (Civil):** Statics, materials science, bridge/road construction. (Source: *OpenStax Physics*, *MIT OCW Civil Engineering*).
  - **Engineering II (Mechanical/Energy):** Thermodynamics, engine repair, hydro/solar/wind power generation. (Source: *OSE Power Cube*, *MIT OCW Energy*).
  - **Chemistry & Manufacturing:** Metal smelting, concrete production, polymer synthesis, basic chemical safety. (Source: *OSE Micromining*, *Open Source Pharma*).
  - **Electronics:** Circuit theory, motor repair, communication systems (radio). (Source: *ARRL Handbook*, *OpenStax Physics*).
- **AI Role:** Simulation engine ("Calculate load bearing..."), troubleshooting guide ("Engine won't start, check...").

### Tier 4: Advancement (The "New Civilization")
*Goal: Restore advanced science, governance, and culture.*
- **Subjects:**
  - **Advanced Medicine:** Surgery, epidemiology, public health systems. (Source: *WHO Advanced Guidelines*, *PubMed Central Archive*).
  - **Higher Sciences:** Quantum physics, biotechnology, advanced mathematics. (Source: *MIT OCW*, *arXiv.org Archive*).
  - **Governance & Ethics:** Law, economics, conflict resolution, sustainable development. (Source: *UNESCO ESD Curriculum*, *Universal Declaration of Human Rights*).
  - **Arts & Humanities:** History, philosophy, music, literature (to preserve cultural soul).
- **AI Role:** Research partner, debate opponent, ethical advisor.

## 3. Pedagogical Methodology: The AI Tutor
The curriculum is not static; it is delivered via the **Stage 2 Main Core AI** (see `architecture-draft.md`).

- **Socratic Method:** The AI asks questions to guide discovery rather than just providing answers.
- **Adaptive Learning:** Adjusts difficulty based on user performance. If a student fails a quiz on thermodynamics, the AI re-explains using simpler analogies or local examples.
- **Project-Based Learning (PBL):** Every module ends with a practical challenge (e.g., "Build a working water filter," "Diagnose this simulated patient").
- **Certification:** The AI issues digital certificates (signed with the Capsule's key) to validate competence, helping to restore professional trust in the new society.

## 5. Implementation Formats
To ensure resilience, the curriculum is stored in three formats:

1.  **Digital Interactive (High Energy):** Full AI-driven platform with video, quizzes, and simulations. (Requires Stage 2 Core).
2.  **Static Digital (Low Energy):** PDF/EPUB versions of textbooks and manuals, searchable via simple interface. (Can run on Stage 1 Pilot or basic e-reader).
3.  **Analog Backup (Zero Energy):** Critical tables, formulas, and diagrams laser-etched on stainless steel plates (Module 2 of `physical-specs-draft.md`).

## 6. Collaboration Needs (Phase 4)
- **Educators:** To structure the syllabi and validate the pedagogical flow.
- **Subject Matter Experts:** To curate the "Top 100" essential papers/books for each field.
- **AI Specialists:** To fine-tune the LLM for tutoring (Socratic prompting) and quiz generation.
- **Translators:** To ensure core materials are available in the 5 most common global languages (English, Spanish, Mandarin, Arabic, Portuguese).
